# priestess walks

### Walk 1

**Ghost:** Blessings upon your steps. I have just replenished the oil in the lamps for the mother goddesses. Do you seek the quiet of our shrines, or wish to hear of our town?
**You:** What do the temples in Calleva look like?
**Ghost:** Grand square structures dominate the peaceful precinct, constructed with care by local builders. Broad stone steps lead up to the raised portico where worshippers mingle before entering. The outer covered paths keep everyone dry while I perform my daily task of sweeping and tending lamps.
**You:** What do people give to the temples?
**Ghost:** Only gentle, bloodless gifts are laid upon our altars. I bake small loaves of sweet barley bread and gather fresh rosemary and thyme from my garden. A few drops of red wine poured on the stone are enough to please the kind spirits.
**You:** How do you spend your mornings?
**Ghost:** My days begin early with sweeping stone steps and preparing quiet offerings. I walk through the courtyard while the dew is still wet, checking the altars and making sure everything is tidy and peaceful before the first worshippers arrive in the morning.
**You:** What equipment helps you every day?
**Ghost:** Twigs tied tightly to hazel handles form the sturdy brooms I use daily. Bronze pitchers help me carry wash water, while soft linen cloths wipe down light dust from oil lamps. Simple clay pots hold all the lavender water used to scrub altar bases.
**You:** Do you live near the temples?
**Ghost:** You will find my dwelling just a few steps from the sacred shrines. Living so close allows me to watch over the temples through the night and ensure the lamps are safe. It is a humble place, perfect for a quiet keeper.
**You:** What is your daily food like?
**Ghost:** Farewell, traveller. May your journey through Calleva be gentle and safe.

### Walk 2

**Ghost:** It gladdens my heart to see you return to Calleva. The temple courtyards are peaceful today, and fresh sweet herbs rest upon the altars. Have you come to offer another prayer?
**You:** Do you have festivals in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Our feast days are bright and cheerful, filled with laughter, shared stories, and sacred songs. I rise extra early to sweep the steps and ensure we have plenty of sweet incense burning. Everyone, from wealthy merchants to humble labourers, celebrates together.
**You:** Do many travellers stop at your temples?
**Ghost:** We welcome both townsfolk who live just down the street and travellers far from home. Parents come with young children to ask for good health, while traders seek safe passage for their heavy wagons. I greet them all with a smile and offer clean water for their dusty feet.
**You:** What news do people talk about?
**Ghost:** Folk love to share tidings while resting in our courtyard. They speak of the autumn harvest, upcoming festival days, or distant word from the emperor far away. I listen quietly as I sweep, offering a gentle word when someone worries over a sick family member or lost sheep.
**You:** Is there a lot of trading in the town?
**Ghost:** Trade flourishes here because major routes join together within our district. Stalls display shiny bronze coins alongside red pottery and dyed cloth. I only handle small coins when buying lamp oil or fresh salt, but the constant hum of the market shows how lively our community remains throughout the year.
**You:** What items are sold in the market?
**Ghost:** Stalls overflow with red clay dishes, iron knives, warm woollen cloaks, and sacks of dried beans. Smoked fish from the coast sits beside pots of sweet honey gathered from local hives. You can find almost anything required for a comfortable house by visiting the market square at midday.
**You:** What is your daily food like?
**Ghost:** May the gods watch over your path today. Go in peace.

### Walk 3

**Ghost:** You walk these stones once more, friend. I was just tidying my small lodging beside the precinct before the afternoon prayers begin. What brings you back to our shrines?
**You:** Can you tell me about Calleva?
**Ghost:** Calleva is a vibrant, busy town where roads meet from every direction. Wide gravel streets run past thriving market stalls, grand stone buildings, and comfortable courtyard homes. Our square temples stand proudly near the centre, offering quiet refuge to all.
**You:** Who comes to visit the temple precinct?
**Ghost:** People from all walks of life pass through our stone gates. Local farmers bring thanks for good rain, while weary merchants stop after long journeys along our paved roads. Each visitor leaves a small token, perhaps a copper coin or a sprig of rosemary, seeking the blessings of our quiet gods.
**You:** What gossip do you hear in Calleva?
**Ghost:** News arrives with every passing trader who stops to pray at the altars. I hear of fine weather in the east, rain in the hills, or distant events across the seas. I prefer the quiet local news myself, such as a neighbour's new baby or a successful honey harvest.
**You:** Do people talk about the crops and farming?
**Ghost:** Farmers often pray for warm sun or gentle rain to protect their wheat and barley crops. When the harvest is brought in safely, they return to offer thanks with small loaves of fresh bread. Good crops mean everyone in town will eat well through the cold winter months ahead.
**You:** How do the changing seasons affect your work?
**Ghost:** Each season changes my daily duties around the sacred grounds. Summer lets me trim flower garlands under clear blue skies, while autumn brings heavy rains that wash the courtyard clean. Winter forces us indoors, where we keep small braziers lit so travellers can warm their cold hands while praying.
**You:** Do you see dogs or birds in the precinct?
**Ghost:** Walk softly. The blessings of the precinct go with you.

**Ghost:** We honour Sulis Minerva for her wisdom, Mars for protection, and the gentle Mother Goddesses for fruitful fields and healthy children. Worshippers bring simple gifts to show their respect, and we never allow any unkind or bloody rituals here.

**Ghost:** A gentle welcome back to you. The lamps burn brightly still, casting a warm light for all who enter. Come, rest awhile from the bustling streets of our town.
**You:** How are the sacred buildings in the precinct built?
**Ghost:** Tall square temples stand proudly within our sacred wall, built with sturdy stone and timber. Covered outer corridors, called porticoes, surround the inner shrine rooms where sacred statues rest. Worshippers gather under these shaded walkways to pray, sheltered from both summer sun and winter rain.
**You:** What gods do you worship in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Our sacred precinct is home to several kind deities, including Sulis Minerva and the three Mother Goddesses. People from all walks of life come to offer prayers for healing, safety, and good fortune, finding comfort in their divine presence.
**You:** Do you sacrifice animals here?
**Ghost:** We do not practice unkind or bloody rites in this quiet precinct. Instead, we offer round cakes, seasonal fruits, and bundles of sweet-smelling herbs. Worshippers also bring modest bronze coins or small clay figures to show their deep gratitude.
**You:** What is your daily food like?
**Ghost:** Simple food suits my quiet work best. I prepare soft barley grains in a pot, served alongside green herbs, garlic, and fresh goat cheese from local farm stalls. Drinks are always plain, usually cool well water mixed with a small dash of red wine.
**You:** How do you prepare your spelt bread?
**Ghost:** Local bakers bring round loaves of dense spelt bread to our market every morning. I pick wild thyme, rosemary, and sage from the garden beds behind the temple portico to season my soup. These fresh greens make even the plainest dry crust taste delicious.
**You:** What money do folk use in the market?
**Ghost:** May the gods watch over your path today. Go in peace.

### Walk 20

**Ghost:** It gladdens my heart to see you return to Calleva. The temple courtyards are peaceful today, and fresh sweet herbs rest upon the altars. Have you come to offer another prayer?
**You:** How do you spend your mornings?
**Ghost:** Each morning, I rise at dawn to tend the sacred precinct. My daily round includes trimming the wicks of our ceramic lamps, sweeping away fallen leaves, and laying out fresh water and herbs. It is a quiet, comforting routine that I love.
**You:** What happens on a feast day?
**Ghost:** When feast days arrive, the whole precinct fills with colourful garlands and music. Townsfolk wear their finest woollen cloaks, and the smell of roasting grain fills the air. It is a time of sharing food and thanking the gods for our safety.
**You:** What special food do you eat during the festivals?
**Ghost:** Special honeyed bread and roasted grains are the treats we share on these holy days. People bring baskets of fresh summer berries or dried fruits to distribute among the crowd. These simple, sweet foods make our celebrations feel like a true family gathering for all of Calleva.
**You:** Do you have music at the temple?
**Ghost:** On special days, local musicians bring soft wooden flutes and small bronze bells to play. We weave beautiful garlands of ivy, willow, and wild roses to drape over the temple pillars. The pleasant sounds and bright colours make everyone feel welcome.
**You:** Do you sacrifice animals here?
**Ghost:** Only gentle, bloodless gifts are laid upon our altars. I bake small loaves of sweet barley bread and gather fresh rosemary and thyme from my garden. A few drops of red wine poured on the stone are enough to please the kind spirits.
**You:** Which gods do people pray to here?
