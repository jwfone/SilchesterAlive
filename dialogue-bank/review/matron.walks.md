# matron walks

### Walk 1

**Ghost:** Welcome to our cheerful town. The hearth is lit at home, and I must spin my wool soon, but I always have time to greet a visitor to Calleva.
**You:** What is your house like?
**Ghost:** My home is a comfortable Calleva town house featuring a small, sunny garden plot. We gather around the glowing hearth in the main room to eat and talk. It is filled with cozy furnishings, clay pots, and fine woven blankets.
**You:** Is it cold in your town?
**Ghost:** In cold winters we gather near the hearth, while summer brings ripe garden greens. The autumn rains can make our gravel streets muddy, but the spring sunshine always brings the fruit trees in our small garden back to life.
**You:** Where do you get your food in town?
**Ghost:** Visiting the stalls each morning brings fresh greens, garlic, and leeks for our pot. I purchase rich olive oil shipped from far away and small jars of salty fish sauce to season our meals. Bargaining with traders ensures my family dines well without wasting our hard-earned bronze coins.
**You:** Where do you bake the bread every morning?
**Ghost:** Grinding wheat grain between heavy quern stones produces coarse flour for our dough. I mix it with water and salt, shape round loaves, and place them inside the warm domed oven near our hearth. Watching the crust turn golden brown guarantees a hearty breakfast.
**You:** What do you do from morning to night?
**Ghost:** My daily routine keeps me very busy from dawn until dusk. I manage the household, supervise the cooking, spin wool on my drop spindle, and always find time to visit the market and the baths to hear the latest gossip.
**You:** What do you do in the afternoon?
**Ghost:** Do take care. Do come back and visit my garden again soon.

### Walk 2

**Ghost:** Welcome back, dear. You have returned just as I finished baking our daily bread. Step closer and tell me how your stroll through our bustling streets went today.
**You:** Where do you get your food in town?
**Ghost:** Browsing the open-air stalls reveals wonderful food brought from across the region. I pick out fresh vegetables, fill a jug with golden olive oil, and buy jars of fermented fish sauce. Every meal we share relies on the good things brought into our bustling forum market each day.
**You:** How do you pay for things in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Buying goods involves exchanging struck bronze and silver money for whatever supplies we need. Sellers count out change carefully into your hand after agreeing on a reasonable price for their goods. Most merchants know us well, making daily shopping a friendly and simple affair for everyone.
**You:** How much are bronze coins worth?
**Ghost:** Everyday transactions rely entirely upon thick bronze coins stamped with clear emperor profile portraits. They are perfect for buying kitchen herbs, clay cooking pots, or paying small entrance fees at the public baths. My purse stays well stocked with bronze whenever I leave home each morning.
**You:** How do women dress when living in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Linen tunics keep me comfortable beneath my woven stola, which reaches down to my sandals. Before leaving our house for the market, I adjust my palla veil to cover my hair as respectable women do. Brooches pin my cloak tightly against the chilly autumn winds in Calleva.
**You:** How is your long stola made and fastened?
**Ghost:** Spun yarn from local sheep is woven into a wide tube of cloth to make this dress. Metal clasps hold the shoulders securely so the fabric drapes neatly over my tunic without slipping down. It feels comfortable enough for walking through our garden or supervising house chores.
**You:** Why do you spin wool in the garden?
**Ghost:** Do take care. Do come back and visit my garden again soon.
