# child walks

### Walk 1

**Ghost:** Mind the gravel! I was just rolling my hoop down the side street. I am Tertius, the mosaic-maker's boy. What brings you to our corner of the forum today?
**You:** What games do you play in the street?
**Ghost:** Rolling hoops and throwing knucklebones keeps us busy when we are not running errands. We draw game boards right in the gravel with sharp twigs and use small pebbles as counters. It is great fun, so long as the town guard does not chase us away for being too noisy.
**You:** What do you and your friends do together?
**Ghost:** Lucius and Gaius are my best mates, since their fathers work near the forum too. Whenever our chores are finished, we meet up behind the bakery. We trade shiny pebbles, practice rolling iron hoops over the gravel, and cheer whenever someone scores a tricky shot at knucklebones.
**You:** What do people do at the amphitheatre?
**Ghost:** Just beyond the eastern town edge lies our great circular earthen amphitheatre. Crowds head there on holiday afternoons to watch musicians, jugglers, and grand displays. High grassy banks give hundreds of people a clear view down into the gravelled arena floor. The noise of cheering folk carries all the way home.
**You:** What places are there to see in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Calleva is a grand, bustling town packed with gravel streets, busy market stalls, and grand stone columns in the forum! We have loud public baths for swimming, and just outside town lies an earthen amphitheatre where jugglers perform for big crowds.
**You:** Where do coins come from?
**Ghost:** Shop owners take shiny coins minted from copper, bronze, or rare silver. When Father buys stone blocks, he pays in heavy silver pieces from his purse. I usually handle small dark copper coins when buying sweet cakes or salt. Money makes trading much simpler than swapping sacks of wheat.
**You:** How do you hear about things happening elsewhere?
**Ghost:** Farewell! Mind the mortar buckets on your way out.

### Walk 2

**Ghost:** You came back! Father is still chipping little stone tiles upstairs, so I have a spare moment. Shall we talk about street games or my errands around town?
**You:** What is your home like near the forum?
**Ghost:** Up a narrow ladder lies our cozy room, sitting right over the noisy tile workshop. Being so close to the forum means we are near all the action! I can look out and watch shopkeepers opening their stalls before I even finish my breakfast porridge.
**You:** Where do people go to buy things in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Bright market stalls pack our town forum from early morning onwards. People come from nearby farms to buy olive oil, pots, or fine timber. Father sends me through the square to pick up supplies or deliver quick messages. It is always crowded with pack horses, barking dogs, and eager shoppers.
**You:** How do you get your bread?
**Ghost:** My first chore of the day is fetching bread for our breakfast. I take a small copper coin from Father and run to the market stalls before they get too crowded. The smell of the freshly baked wheat loaves guides me right to the ovens.
**You:** Why is there a cross on the bread?
**Ghost:** Flat circular shapes are what come out of the ovens here. The cross slashed across the crust keeps the dough from cracking wildly as it bakes. When I bring a loaf home, Father snaps it right along those lines to share with our workshop helpers.
**You:** How do the bakers bake the bread?
**Ghost:** Big domed stone ovens sit right behind the bakery counter. The bakers build hot wood fires inside them until the bricks glow red, then sweep out the ashes. Long wooden paddles slide the dough inside, and the whole street smells like fresh roasted grain as they cook.
**You:** What do you eat every day?
**Ghost:** See you later! I must run before Father calls for water.

### Walk 3

**Ghost:** Back again? Splendid! I just finished my wax tablet lessons with the tutor, so I am free to chat. What would you like to hear about next?
**You:** What is your name and what is your job here?
**Ghost:** I am Tertius, nine years old and the son of a mosaic-maker. Most days I run all over town hauling buckets of water to mix fresh mortar, fetching tasty loaves, and taking messages across the busy streets. It keeps my legs moving, though my arms get tired!
**You:** Can you show me the tools in your workshop?
**Ghost:** We have many special tools, like bronze dividers for drawing perfect circles in the plaster. Father has a favorite iron hardie, which is a chisel set upright in a block of wood. He strikes stones against it to snap them into neat squares.
**You:** Is carrying water your hardest job in the workshop?
**Ghost:** To make the thick paste that holds our mosaic floors together, I must carry fresh water from the town well. My shoulders ache from the wooden yoke, but Father says a perfect mix is the secret to a floor that lasts for a hundred years or more.
**You:** What are those tiny little stone squares called?
**Ghost:** We use thousands of tiny stone cubes called tesserae to create our mosaic pictures. I help sort them by colour into wooden trays so Father can reach them quickly. He taps each one into the mortar with a small wooden mallet to keep them flat.
**You:** Is the forum busy every day?
**Ghost:** Traders sell almost everything in the forum, from salty fish to shiny bronze brooches. Stone columns line the sides where shade protects the busy stalls. Whenever I run errands, I pass farmers hawking fresh greens and cobblers mending old sandals. The chatter never really stops until the sun sets completely.
**You:** Who are the traders in the square?
**Ghost:** See you later! I must run before Father calls for water.

**Ghost:** Our grand basilica stands on the northern side of the forum square, filled with massive stone columns. That is where the town magistrates hold court and discuss important business. It is so big inside that your voice echoes off the high timber roof.
**You:** Who are the traders in the square?
**Ghost:** Farewell! Mind the mortar buckets on your way out.

### Walk 19

**Ghost:** Hello friend! Did you hear the jugglers at the amphitheatre today? I am just running water to Father's workshop, but I can linger for a quick talk.
**You:** What is your home like near the forum?
**Ghost:** We live in a simple upper room right above Father's mosaic workshop, just a quick hop from the forum. It gets a bit dusty from the stone cutting below, but I love hearing the chatter of the market people through our window every morning.
**You:** Who do you live with?
**Ghost:** Father is the master of our workshop, designing grand tile pictures for villas. He has taught me to recognize all the different coloured stones we use. We work side by side every single day, and he always buys me a sweet honey cake when we have a successful week.
**You:** Can you show me the tools in your workshop?
**Ghost:** We have many special tools, like bronze dividers for drawing perfect circles in the plaster. Father has a favorite iron hardie, which is a chisel set upright in a block of wood. He strikes stones against it to snap them into neat squares.
**You:** How do you make the tiles square?
**Ghost:** Our small pointed hammer is the most important tool we own. It has sharp, hardened steel tips at both ends. Watching Father tap-tap-tap along a line of red brick is amazing; he never misses and makes perfect square tesserae every single time.
**You:** What is a plumb-bob used for?
**Ghost:** We use a heavy bronze plumb-bob hanging from a wooden frame to check our levels. If the pointed lead weight does not line up with the center mark, the floor is slanted. Customers get very angry if their expensive mosaic floors are bumpy!
**You:** Where do you get the stones for your mosaics?
**Ghost:** Goodbye! Come back soon for another game of knucklebones.

### Walk 20

**Ghost:** You came back! Father is still chipping little stone tiles upstairs, so I have a spare moment. Shall we talk about street games or my errands around town?
**You:** What do you do when you are not working?
**Ghost:** When Father gives me some free time, I meet the other boys by the side streets. We play a lively game of ball, tossing it high over the timber roofs. We also roll wooden hoops using short sticks, steering them around the sleepy dogs snoozing in the warm dust.
**You:** Who are your best friends in town?
**Ghost:** Lucius and Gaius are my best mates, since their fathers work near the forum too. Whenever our chores are finished, we meet up behind the bakery. We trade shiny pebbles, practice rolling iron hoops over the gravel, and cheer whenever someone scores a tricky shot at knucklebones.
**You:** What is the forum like in your town?
**Ghost:** Shouting merchants fill the open forum square right near our home. You can find butchers, potters, and cloth sellers trading under giant stone porticos. Folk gather there every morning to chat, trade, and hear official news. It is the noisiest, brightest part of town, full of rich smells and bustling crowds.
**You:** Can you tell me about the town of Calleva?
**Ghost:** Calleva is a grand, bustling town packed with gravel streets, busy market stalls, and grand stone columns in the forum! We have loud public baths for swimming, and just outside town lies an earthen amphitheatre where jugglers perform for big crowds.
**You:** Why do people go to the town baths?
**Ghost:** Townsfolk gather at the public baths to wash off grime and relax after work. You hear loud splashing, echoes, and constant shouting the moment you step inside. People scrape their skin with oil, take hot plunges, and gossip about local trade. It is as much for meeting friends as staying clean.
**You:** Where is the amphitheatre located?
**Ghost:** See you later! I must run before Father calls for water.
