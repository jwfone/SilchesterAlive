# magistrate walks

### Walk 1

**Ghost:** Greetings, traveller. You find me on my way to the basilica to oversee civic business. Our town flourishes through good order and duty. How can I help you on your visit?
**You:** What is your name and what do you do here?
**Ghost:** You speak with Gaius Julius Vitalis, a proud member of Calleva's town council. I dedicate my days to civic duties, organising the taxes and public works. It is my responsibility to make sure our lovely town runs smoothly and stays prosperous.
**You:** What is a typical day like for you?
**Ghost:** Each morning begins with quiet prayers at my home shrine, followed by council business at the basilica. After working through the midday heat, I visit the public baths to bathe and chat with friends. Finally, I return home to enjoy a hearty evening dinner with my family.
**You:** What kind of food do people have here?
**Ghost:** We eat very well here, enjoying local spelt bread, thick barley porridge, and fresh garden cabbages. For meats, we have beef, pork, and sometimes wild venison from the forests. We wash this down with wine imported from the warm southern provinces.
**You:** Tell me about the town market.
**Ghost:** Traders and farmers gather in the busy forum square to sell their goods from colourful stalls. You can buy fresh cabbages, local woollen cloaks, iron tools, and beautiful pottery from distant lands. The air is always filled with the shouting of merchants boasting of their bargains.
**You:** Are there blacksmiths in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Ironmongery is vital for our town's growth, providing the nails and brackets that hold our timber houses together. Our local smiths also fashion excellent shearing tools for wool and sharp sickles for the harvest. You can hear their hammers ringing near the market daily.
**You:** Do you see many beasts in Calleva?
**Ghost:** Good day to you. Do enjoy the bustling market and our fine public baths.

### Walk 2

**Ghost:** Good to see you once more in Calleva. I have just finished inspecting the local street drains near the market square. What else would you like to know about our fine town?
**You:** Can you describe your house to me?
**Ghost:** You will find my residence near the great basilica, a fine house with thick stone walls. We enjoy a private courtyard where my children play, away from the noisy streets. It is a peaceful, comfortable home filled with fine Roman furnishings.
**You:** What are the streets like?
**Ghost:** Orderly rows of houses line our gravelled streets, which we lay out in a clean, square pattern. It is a modern way of building that lets carts pass easily. Maintaining these gravel ways is one of our most important civic duties.
**You:** How do you keep the streets from flooding?
**Ghost:** Open gutters cut beside the gravel paths gather rainwater and carry it smoothly out of town. We lay heavy timber slabs over the drains outside doorways to create clean stepping places for pedestrians. Maintaining these channels prevents dampness from ruining the foundations of our clay and timber buildings.
**You:** How do you look after the town of Calleva?
**Ghost:** Our council duties are vital for keeping Calleva safe and prosperous. We oversee the collection of grain taxes, which are stored safely in our granaries. We also pay for the upkeep of our magnificent basilica and ensure the town's drains flow properly.
**You:** How do you repair the town streets?
**Ghost:** Crews of labourers are paid from civic tax funds to haul fresh gravel from local pits into town. They spread and ram the stones tight to keep our grid streets firm under heavy wagon wheels. Maintaining smooth thoroughfares is essential for keeping trade moving into the forum.
**You:** Who collects the taxes?
**Ghost:** Until we meet again. May the gods grant you safe travel and good fortune.

**Ghost:** We are never truly isolated here, as visitors arrive weekly with tales from the southern ports. They bring news of imperial victories and shipping across the channel. Knowing we are part of such a vast, orderly realm fills us with pride.

### Walk 4

**You:** Who lives with you in your house?
**Ghost:** Within my stone house, you will find my loving family and the servants who assist us. The servants are essential for running a large home, handling everything from cooking to sweeping. Together, we form a very orderly and peaceful household in the heart of our busy town.
**You:** What do you do every day?
**Ghost:** My day is filled with civic duties and pleasant social habits. I spend my mornings at the forum, helping to manage our town's taxes and streets. When the afternoon arrives, I relax at the public baths before walking home for a peaceful family meal in our dining room.
**You:** What are your main duties as a councillor?
**Ghost:** We councillors meet regularly to decide on important matters for Calleva. Our main task is collecting taxes in coin and grain to fund our public works. We use these funds to repair the gravelled streets, clear the drains, and keep our public buildings clean.
**You:** Who looks after the water drains?
**Ghost:** Street drains require constant sweeping to stop waste blocking the flow of water during winter downpours. The council allocates funds to clean the culverts that lead away from the public baths and forum. Keeping these channels clear protects our stone foundations and maintains public health.
**You:** How do you pay taxes?
**Ghost:** Farewell for now. May your journey through Calleva be pleasant and peaceful.

**You:** What do your children learn?
**Ghost:** My children are taught reading, writing, and arithmetic by a tutor here in town. Learning Latin letters and basic numbers is necessary for managing property and understanding council business later in life. We also teach them proper respect for the gods, civic duty, and elders.

**Ghost:** Farewell for now. May your journey through Calleva be pleasant and peaceful.
